# Studyboard

A calm place to keep track of homework, tests and grades.

**Open it:** https://london3400.github.io/studyboard/

On iPhone: open the link in Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, then open Studyboard from your home screen and pick your class.

- The school's test calendar and lunch menu are downloaded automatically a few times a day.
- Your own homework and grades are saved only on your own device. Nothing about you is stored here.
